# SafeSphere AI — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 18, 2026

## 1. About SafeSphere AI

SafeSphere AI is a social media safety platform prototype designed to help Facebook Page managers explore safer online community management.

For privacy-related questions, contact **shajedulkabir106@gmail.com**.

## 2. Information We Process

When you register, SafeSphere AI stores your account information, including your email address, display name, password hash, workspace information, and authentication session data.

If you connect Facebook, SafeSphere AI receives the Facebook Pages you authorize, their Page identifiers and names, permitted Page access credentials, granted permissions, and published Page post metadata such as post identifiers, captions, timestamps, and available media links.

The current prototype does not intentionally retrieve personal Facebook timelines, private messages, Facebook friends, or real Facebook Page comments.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use account information to authenticate users and provide their workspaces.

We use authorized Facebook Page information to list connected Pages and retrieve their published posts.

The simulated harassment-review feature creates fictional comments, fictional account identities, and scripted severity labels. These are demonstration records, not information collected from real Facebook commenters.

## 4. Facebook Authorization

Facebook connection is performed using Meta's official authorization process.

Facebook Page access credentials are encrypted before storage in the application's database. They are not exposed to the frontend as plaintext access tokens.

Users should authorize only Pages they have permission to manage.

## 5. Storage and Security

Account, workspace, authorized Page, post, and simulated-scan data are stored in the application's database.

We implement access controls and encrypted storage for Facebook Page credentials. No internet-connected application can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Sharing

SafeSphere AI uses Meta's APIs to access Facebook Page information authorized by users.

We do not sell personal information. Application hosting and database providers may process data as necessary to operate the service.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as it is reasonably necessary to operate the account or fulfill legitimate operational and legal requirements.

Users may request deletion of their SafeSphere AI account, stored Facebook Page connections, cached posts, and associated demonstration data by contacting **shajedulkabir106@gmail.com**.

Deletion requests are verified before processing. Certain records may need to be retained when legally required or temporarily remain in backups until their normal deletion cycle.

## 8. User Rights and Choices

Users can choose whether to authorize Facebook Pages and may disconnect Page connections in SafeSphere AI. Users may also revoke Meta authorizations through Facebook's settings.

For account information, privacy concerns, or deletion requests, contact **shajedulkabir106@gmail.com**.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. The current version will be published on this page.
