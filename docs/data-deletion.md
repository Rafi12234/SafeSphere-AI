# SafeSphere AI — User Data Deletion Instructions

SafeSphere AI users may request deletion of personal information stored by the application. **The current prototype does not provide an automated account-deletion button or a complete automated deletion endpoint.** The process described below requires action by the SafeSphere AI team.

## How to Request Deletion

**Step 1:** Send an email to **shajedulkabir106@gmail.com** with the subject:

**SafeSphere AI — Data Deletion Request**

**Step 2:** Identify the email address associated with your SafeSphere AI account. Do not send your Facebook password, Facebook access token, or other secrets.

**Step 3:** Our team will verify that the request is from the account owner and arrange deletion of eligible account and associated data. We will communicate about the request through the support email channel. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other secrets as proof of identity.

## Information Covered

Depending on the account and applicable retention obligations, this may include:

- SafeSphere AI account and workspace data
- Stored Facebook Page connections and encrypted credentials
- Cached Facebook Page posts
- Associated simulated scan records and fictional demonstration data

## Disconnecting Facebook Access

You may disconnect a Facebook Page using the SafeSphere AI dashboard. You may separately revoke SafeSphere AI's access from your Facebook settings.

Disconnecting a Page removes the Page's locally stored credential. It is **not** the same as deleting your entire SafeSphere AI account or all previously cached Page posts; request full deletion separately by email.

## Important Information

Facebook controls information stored on Facebook itself. Deleting information from SafeSphere AI does not delete your Facebook profile or Facebook Page.

We may need to retain limited information when required by law or until existing backups expire under the applicable retention process.

For questions or confirmation of a deletion request, contact **shajedulkabir106@gmail.com**.
